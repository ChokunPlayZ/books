# Library workflows

- Browse by series, search, filter, and see all owned volumes in an edition.
- Add books or use the short **Add volume** form with recent series defaults.
- Edit series name, type and publisher from **Edit title details** on the title page. Changes apply to every owned volume in the matching edition; separate editions keep their own type and publisher. Volume forms inherit these details and let you select an existing edition or add another one.
- Edit reading status and purchase details; calculate percent/amount discounts.
- Ctrl/Cmd-click covers to toggle selection, or Shift-click to select a range in the current order. **Select series/books** also enables selection by touch or keyboard. Select all filtered results, change reading status together, or remove/restore selected books. Selecting a series includes its matching volumes; changing filters or sorting clears the selection. Escape leaves selection mode.
- Choose **Edit volumes** on a series page to switch to a table of every owned volume’s purchase date, price and store. A bottom-centered bar shows unsaved changes: **Save changes** applies them together, while **Discard changes** returns to the covers without saving. Blank prices stay distinct from zero, and leaving the page with unsaved edits prompts a warning.
- Remove and restore books through **Recently removed**.
- Scan ISBNs from photos or a live camera, recognize printed ISBNs, and use a USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner.
- Check ownership by equivalent ISBN-10/ISBN-13 and link ISBNs to existing volumes.
- Show whole-volume shelf gaps by publisher and language, combining colored and regular manga without inventing fractional extras.
- Open **What’s missing** for every unowned volume in your series’ publisher catalogs, including newer volumes beyond your shelf, alongside shelf gaps. Catalogs load automatically with up to three checks at once. Alternate listings and gaps count each volume once; languages and publishers stay separate. Search, sort, add a missing volume, or refresh the catalogs. **Catalog coverage** identifies unavailable sources, incomplete checks and editions that need review on their series page.
- Count regular and single-volume special editions toward the same volume; group alternate listings for missing volumes and review multi-volume sets separately.
- Check full series catalogs, choose publisher categories, and review uncertain editions. Automatic publisher results skip Special Set listings, including Thai special-set labels.
- Browse Phoenix Next, First Page Pro, Luckpim, Siam Inter Comics (Yaakz) and Yen Press releases; filter manga/light novels and special editions; save releases for later. Yen Press shows the current month’s print releases.
- Use **Discover titles** to browse Phoenix Next or First Page Pro’s navigation, Yaakz’s product catalog, or **Yen Press · All titles**, including Phoenix GL and First Page Pro’s General/18+/20+ manga categories. Each remaining title shows one representative book, preferring regular volume 1. First Page Pro manga and novels have separate cards and filters; owning one format leaves the other available to discover. Yen Press manga/comics and novels also have separate cards and English format labels.
- Enter a complete ISBN in Discover’s search while browsing Phoenix Next or First Page Pro to search the publisher’s full catalog, including books already on your shelf. This also works in Releases with the corresponding publisher selected. Phoenix results require a matching product barcode. First Page Pro searches its ISBN index without publishing the ISBN on its product pages: results identify this limitation, and adding a result carries your entered ISBN into the review form for checking before saving.
- See covers on series, owned volumes, missing catalog volumes and releases. Covers load as needed from publisher catalogs or verified ISBN metadata, with a placeholder when no image is available.
- Open a volume’s three-dot menu to edit metadata, change its cover, or visit the publisher. **Pull covers** reads matching editions and their product galleries from Phoenix Next, First Page Pro, Luckpim, Yaakz and Yen Press, and checks Naiin and Yaakz bookstore listings; select an image and save. You can also enter a supported image URL and publisher product link. Choices are stored per copy, survive refreshes and restarts, and travel with JSON backups. Clear the image URL to return to automatic covers.
- Look up exact ISBN metadata using Naiin, Yaakz, Yen Press, then Google Books and Open Library. Yen Press supplies English manga and novel details, imprint names and covers; **Pull covers** also checks Yen Press for English editions with an ISBN.

Publisher requests run on the Next.js server using native asynchronous fetch. Catalog results are cached in SQLite; failed refreshes retain the previous successful result with its original check time and a stale warning. Catalog checks remain independent of recent release listings. Photos are decoded on the device and never uploaded to the app or metadata providers.

Discovery reads publisher navigation or Yen Press’s series index first, then checks title categories as their cards come into view (at most three checks concurrently). Duplicate category links share one title card; First Page Pro and Yen Press manga and novel editions remain separate. Directories are reused for seven days and title catalogs for 24 hours, with successful results retained for 30 days; **Refresh titles** checks them again. Scraping features share cached pages and concurrent requests. Failed requests wait 15 minutes before another attempt, including manual refreshes. Opening the cover picker reuses catalogs and product galleries. When volume 1 is absent, the card identifies the available volume. Phoenix, First Page Pro and Yen Press title categories with no current book listings are hidden once the category check confirms they are empty. Failed or incomplete checks remain visible so they can be retried; unnumbered books and series whose first available book is a later volume still appear. Cover images are validated and stored as image bytes in SQLite, with a permanent content-based `coverId`. The browser loads `/api/covers/<id>` through the signed-in library; image requests read the database without contacting publishers. Identical images share one ID. Saved images survive restarts and source outages, and JSON backups include their bytes. Cover matching and source lookups run on the server; the browser has no cover cache or catalog-matching rules. Standard private browser caching uses immutable IDs and ETags.

Yaakz provides paginated public product data and Nuxt product details. Products containing several volumes are expanded into their individual variants, each with its own ISBN, image and product URL; a parent range image is never assigned to every volume. Bookstore covers require an exact ISBN, or a matching series, volume, publisher/format where available. Cover choices show their source, and source failures leave successful choices available.

Yen Press discovery uses its public series index and loads each series’ print catalog as needed. Yen Press, Yen On, Ize Press and JY use the same catalog source. Series comparisons include older volumes from continuation pages and support pasted `https://yenpress.com/series/…` links. English format suffixes such as “(comic)” and “(light novel)” are ignored when comparing names; language and format remain separate. Automatic covers also work for numbered Yen Press volumes without a recorded ISBN. Release prices are left blank because Yen Press lists US/Canadian prices and this library records amounts in baht.
