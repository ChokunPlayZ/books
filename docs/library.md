# Library workflows

- Browse by series, search, filter, and see all owned volumes in an edition.
- Add books or use the short **Add volume** form with recent series defaults.
- Edit series name, type and publisher from **Edit title details** on the title page. Changes apply to every owned volume in the matching edition; separate editions keep their own type and publisher. Volume forms inherit these details and let you select an existing edition or add another one.
- Edit reading status and purchase details; calculate percent/amount discounts.
- Ctrl/Cmd-click covers to toggle selection, or Shift-click to select a range in the current order. **Select series/books** also enables selection by touch or keyboard. Select all filtered results, change reading status together, or remove/restore selected books. Selecting a series includes its matching volumes; changing filters or sorting clears the selection. Escape leaves selection mode.
- Choose **Edit volumes** on a series page to switch to a table of every owned volume’s purchase date, price and store. A bottom-centered bar shows unsaved changes: **Save changes** applies them together, while **Discard changes** returns to the covers without saving. Blank prices stay distinct from zero, and leaving the page with unsaved edits prompts a warning.
- Remove and restore books through **Recently removed**.
- To fix a series split across different names, choose **Select series**, select the entries, then **Merge series**. Review every owned volume in the preview and choose the name to keep. Merging includes volumes hidden by filters and preserves each copy’s edition, ISBN, cover, reading status and purchase details. Catalog choices under the kept name take priority for overlapping editions. A changed series must be refreshed and selected again before merging.
- Scan ISBNs from photos or a live camera, recognize printed ISBNs, and use a USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner.
- Check ownership by equivalent ISBN-10/ISBN-13 and link ISBNs to existing volumes.
- Show whole-volume shelf gaps by publisher and language, combining colored and regular manga without inventing fractional extras.
- Open **What’s missing** for every unowned volume in your series’ publisher catalogs, including newer volumes beyond your shelf, alongside shelf gaps. Catalogs load automatically with up to three checks at once. Alternate listings and gaps count each volume once; languages and publishers stay separate. Search, sort, add a missing volume, or refresh the catalogs. **Catalog coverage** identifies unavailable sources, incomplete checks and editions that need review on their series page.
- Count regular and single-volume special editions toward the same volume; group alternate listings for missing volumes and review multi-volume sets separately.
- Check full series catalogs, choose publisher categories, and review uncertain editions. Automatic publisher results skip Special Set listings, including Thai special-set labels.
- Browse Animag, Phoenix Next, First Page Pro, Luckpim, Siam Inter Comics (Yaakz) and Yen Press releases; filter manga/light novels and special editions; save releases for later. Yen Press shows the current month’s print releases.
- Use **Discover titles** to browse Animag’s five book imprints, Phoenix Next or First Page Pro’s navigation, Yaakz’s product catalog, or **Yen Press · All titles**, including Phoenix GL and First Page Pro’s General/18+/20+ manga categories. Each remaining title shows one representative book, preferring regular volume 1. First Page Pro manga and novels have separate cards and filters; owning one format leaves the other available to discover. Yen Press manga/comics and novels also have separate cards and English format labels.
- Enter a complete ISBN in Discover’s search while browsing Animag, Phoenix Next or First Page Pro to search the publisher’s full catalog, including books already on your shelf. This also works in Releases with the corresponding publisher selected. Animag and Phoenix results require matching product ISBN details. First Page Pro searches its ISBN index without publishing the ISBN on its product pages: results identify this limitation, and adding a result carries your entered ISBN into the review form for checking before saving.
- See covers on series, owned volumes, missing catalog volumes and releases. Covers load as needed from publisher catalogs or verified ISBN metadata, with a placeholder when no image is available.
- Open a volume’s three-dot menu to edit metadata, change its cover, or visit the publisher. **Pull covers** reads matching editions and their product galleries from Animag, Phoenix Next, First Page Pro, Luckpim, Yaakz and Yen Press, and checks Naiin and Yaakz bookstore listings; select an image and save. You can also enter a supported image URL and publisher product link. Choices are stored per copy, survive refreshes and restarts, and travel with JSON backups. Clear the image URL to return to automatic covers.
- Look up exact ISBN metadata using Naiin, Yaakz, First Page Pro, Animag, Phoenix Next, Yen Press and Kinokuniya, then Google Books and Open Library. Yen Press supplies English manga and novel details, imprint names and covers; **Pull covers** also checks Yen Press for English editions with an ISBN.

Publisher requests run on the Next.js server using native asynchronous fetch. Catalog results are cached in SQLite; failed refreshes retain the previous successful result with its original check time and a stale warning. Catalog checks remain independent of recent release listings. Photos are decoded on the device and never uploaded to the app or metadata providers.

Discovery reads publisher navigation or Yen Press’s series index first, then checks title categories as their cards come into view (at most three checks concurrently). Duplicate category links share one title card; First Page Pro and Yen Press manga and novel editions remain separate. Directories are reused for seven days and title catalogs for 24 hours, with successful results retained for 30 days; **Refresh titles** checks them again. Scraping features share cached pages and concurrent requests. Failed requests wait 15 minutes before another attempt, including manual refreshes. Opening the cover picker reuses catalogs and product galleries. When volume 1 is absent, the card identifies the available volume. Animag, Phoenix, First Page Pro and Yen Press title categories with no current book listings are hidden once the category check confirms they are empty. Failed or incomplete checks remain visible so they can be retried; unnumbered books and series whose first available book is a later volume still appear. Cover images are validated and stored as image bytes in SQLite, with a permanent content-based `coverId`. The browser loads `/api/covers/<id>` through the signed-in library; image requests read the database without contacting publishers. Identical images share one ID. Saved images survive restarts and source outages, and JSON backups include their bytes. Cover matching and source lookups run on the server; the browser has no cover cache or catalog-matching rules. Standard private browser caching uses immutable IDs and ETags.

Yaakz provides paginated public product data and Nuxt product details. Products containing several volumes are expanded into their individual variants, each with its own ISBN, image and product URL; a parent range image is never assigned to every volume. Bookstore covers require an exact ISBN, or a matching series, volume, publisher/format where available. Cover choices show their source, and source failures leave successful choices available.

Yen Press discovery uses its public series index and loads each series’ print catalog as needed. Yen Press, Yen On, Ize Press and JY use the same catalog source. Series comparisons include older volumes from continuation pages and support pasted `https://yenpress.com/series/…` links. English format suffixes such as “(comic)” and “(light novel)” are ignored when comparing names; language and format remain separate. Automatic covers also work for numbered Yen Press volumes without a recorded ISBN. Release prices are left blank because Yen Press lists US/Canadian prices and this library records amounts in baht.

Animag supports A-Plus, G-Plus, Animag Books, Animag Comics and BeLOVED+ publisher names. Browse all titles or an imprint, check a full series category for missing volumes, or search an ISBN in Discover or Releases. Older selectable products expand into their listed variants, preserving each volume’s ISBN, cover and link; bundles remain marked for review. Publisher book links and available cover metadata carry into the add-book form.

## Anime adaptations and seasonal books

On a series page, **Anime adaptations** checks AniList’s book records for explicitly linked anime and shows their status, season, and AniList/MyAnimeList links. Exact title matches are shown directly; other results are labelled as possible matches. Search by the original, English, or romaji title when a translated shelf title does not match. The book-type menu separates manga and light novels. A missing link means AniList has no direct adaptation recorded for that entry.

In **Discover → Browse → Current anime season · Thai editions**, browse Thai manga matched to seasonal anime. The menu starts on the current calendar season and **Matched editions**, with season/year controls, book-type and anime-status filters, and Thai/English/Japanese/romaji title search. Choose **All linked books** to include books without a confirmed Thai listing, or switch the type to light novels. Source material is labelled separately from manga or novel adaptations. Titles with no linked book are omitted. Ongoing anime that started in an earlier season remain under their original season.

Thai matches use Animag, Phoenix Next and First Page Pro’s title directories, comparing whole multilingual aliases and catalog URL names, then checking matching categories for an actual Thai listing of the correct format. Sequels, manga and light novels remain separate; ambiguous aliases are omitted. Each match shows the Thai title, publisher, representative volume, availability, matching evidence and publisher link. **Add Thai edition** opens the add-book dialog with the listing’s title, type, volume, publisher, ISBN when supplied, and cover/link metadata. Review the purchase details before saving. An edition already on your shelf links to that series. “Matched” confirms a catalog listing, not current stock; catalog coverage and previous checks are shown separately. Unmatched titles may still have Thai editions from other publishers or under other names.

For unmatched books, **Pick this book** opens the ordinary add-book dialog with its title and type. Choose the volume, language, publisher and purchase details before saving. Discovery does not invent ISBNs or publisher editions.

AniList data is fetched on the server through its [public GraphQL API](https://docs.anilist.co/guide/graphql/queries/media); no API key is needed. Seasonal results are cached for six hours and title searches for seven days. Previous successful results remain available during outages and are labelled with their original check time. AniList supplies the metadata; MyAnimeList links use AniList’s corresponding MAL IDs.
