# meemai

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Personal manga and light novel collector. Primarily enters and checks books on a handheld device; desktop is primarily for viewing.

## Product Purpose

meemai takes its name from the Thai มีมั้ย (“Do you have it?”). Use lowercase **meemai** in English and **มีมั้ย** in the Thai interface.

Maintain owned volumes, identify missing volumes, discover releases, and track reading and spending.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve collection CRUD, ISBN lookup and scanning, publisher catalogs, discovery, missing volumes, series editing, bulk selection, statistics, import/export, authentication, and light/dark themes. Existing Next.js 16.4 and React 19 application backed by PostgreSQL.

## Product Principles

Prioritize handheld entry and thumb reach. Keep desktop browsing comfortable. Reduce decorative chrome and generic dashboard patterns. Preserve real collection data and workflows.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing routes, components, unit/integration/browser suites, and screenshots under outputs/. No fabricated product claims or collection data.
